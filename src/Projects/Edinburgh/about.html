Who we are

Apartments, presented with care

Edinburgh Royale Apartment manages and offers multiple apartments for short-term stays. This website exists to show each apartment clearly, with its photographs, amenities and details, so you can choose the one that suits you.

When you are ready, each apartment links to its own Airbnb listing, where booking is completed. If you would rather speak to us first, you can send an enquiry on WhatsApp.
